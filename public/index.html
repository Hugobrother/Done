Questions

What does it work with?

The Claude desktop app on Mac and Windows, and claude.ai in Chrome, Safari, Edge, Brave and Firefox on Mac, Windows and Linux.

What do I need on my phone?

The free ntfy app (iPhone or Android). No account. Scan the QR code shown by done. and your phone is connected.

First launch is blocked?

Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › “Open Anyway”. Windows: “More info” › “Run anyway”. Linux: chmod +x done-linux, then restart your browser once.

Can I make alerts silent or change the text?

Yes. Each alert can be loud or silent, with your own words, and only for long answers if you want.
