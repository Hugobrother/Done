Questions

Does done. read my conversations?

No. It only checks whether the stop button is on screen. Nothing you write leaves your Mac; your phone gets a one-line ping.

What does it work with?

The Claude desktop app on Mac and Windows, and claude.ai in Chrome, Safari, Edge, Brave and Firefox on Mac, Windows and Linux. In a browser, keep the Claude tab as the active tab of its window.

Do I need to install anything on my phone?

Just ntfy, a free and open-source notification app (iPhone and Android). No account. done. sets up your private channel when you scan the QR code.

I don't want a ping for every short reply.

In Settings, set a minimum duration, for example 120 seconds. Approval alerts always come through.

Can I make alerts silent?

Yes. Each alert can be loud or silent, and you can write your own text for each one.
